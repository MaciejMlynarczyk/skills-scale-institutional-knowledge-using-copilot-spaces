# OctoAcme — Risk Register Template

Use this template to keep risk tracking consistent across projects. Keep entries concise and update weekly (or sooner when risk status changes).

## Risk Register Table

| ID | Risk Description | Impact (H/M/L) | Likelihood (H/M/L) | Owner | Mitigation / Contingency | Status | Target Review Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-001 | Example: External API rate limit may block launch traffic | High | Medium | Project Manager | Coordinate quota increase with provider; implement retry/backoff fallback | Open | 2026-05-01 |

## Update Cadence Checklist
- [ ] New risks logged during planning and weekly syncs
- [ ] Impact/likelihood reviewed with PM + PdM + delivery lead
- [ ] Owners assigned for all active risks
- [ ] Mitigation actions tracked with due dates
- [ ] Closed risks archived with a short resolution note
